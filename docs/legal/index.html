This website

Privacy. This site runs no analytics or ads. Its only cookie remembers the language you pick in the menu at the top, and only once you pick one. Fonts and images are served from this site. Links to stores and other sites follow those sites' own policies.

Language. When you first arrive, the site uses your country (from your connection) and your browser's language to choose which language to show. Nothing about this is stored.

Contact form. When you send the form, your name, email address and message are emailed to us so we can reply, and are kept only in our inbox. Nothing is stored on the website. The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to stop spam, which loads a script from Cloudflare and checks your browser (Cloudflare privacy policy). Ask us any time to delete your messages.

Trailers. A game page shows its trailer as a picture from this site. Only when you press play does the video load from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (Google privacy policy).
