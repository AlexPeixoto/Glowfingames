Fly a lantern ship through nine hand-built sectors and take on 39 bosses.

STARKEEL is a vertical space shooter made for phones. Your ship fires on its own. You drag anywhere on the screen to move, so your thumb never covers the ship, and you dodge orange bullets while your build grows. Strong builds can almost play themselves.

Features

Why you'll keep playing

  • Easy to pick up

    Drag anywhere to steer, firing is automatic. Only the small dot in the middle of your ship can be hit, and every enemy always moves and shoots the same way, so you learn a pattern once.

  • Crosslink combos

    Each weapon leaves its own mark. Hit a marked enemy with a different weapon and the two combine into a combo. 14 weapons, 10 lenses and 15 pairings to discover.

  • Nine sectors, 39 bosses

    Each sector has its own look, music, enemies and hazards, two mid-bosses and a sector boss with several acts. Pick a route and find the hidden bonus bosses.

  • The Workshop

    Bring back scrap and cores to unlock hulls, pilots, parts and research between runs. Earn stars to open the next sectors.

  • Made to replay

    A fresh layout every run, plus Endless, Gauntlet, Boss Rush, Daily Seed and Weekly Challenge modes, Heat mutators and 58 trophies.

  • Free to try

    The tutorial and the first two sectors are free. One purchase unlocks the full game and your progress carries over. No ads, plays offline.
